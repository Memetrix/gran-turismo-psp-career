Save patched ISO

Keep this page open until your ISO is ready. Once saved, open it in PPSSPP or copy it to your PSP's ISO folder. You'll need your own unmodified game: the USA version 1.00 or 2.00, or the European version 2.00 (UCES01245).

The European version has the career in the disc's eight languages: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese and Russian. It reads your USA career save the first time it starts with no save of its own. On a PSP, GT Career Save Converter from the release page copies saves either way.

Supported images & checks

USA ISO versions 1.00 and 2.00 are supported: CRC32 9613AC93, 1EADB6B4 or 71DCC467. The European version 2.00 (UCES01245) is supported: CRC32 DA79C7C1. The patch download and finished ISO are checked before you can save it. The finished 1.1.1 ISO has CRC32 709DCC77 (USA) or DE8DDC32 (Europe). Selecting an already patched ISO checks it without applying the patch again.
