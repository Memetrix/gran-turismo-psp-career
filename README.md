# Gran Turismo PSP Career 1.1.1

Gran Turismo PSP came out in 2009 with more than 800 cars and no career mode. Gran Turismo PSP Career is a free fan patch that adds one inside the game. It also brings back five tracks that were cut from the game, and adds 32 cars.

## Career

- **289 events in nine halls**, from Beginner to Endurance. 125 are the classic Gran Turismo 4 events, from the Sunday Cup to the Gran Turismo World Championship. The other 164 are new.
- **74 championships** with the same rivals all season.
- **18 endurance races** up to 24 hours, saved in segments.
- **Prize cars from 262 events.**
- **Rally, dirt and snow** events.
- **Licences** as in GT4, earned in GT PSP's own Driving Challenge.
- **Every dealer is open every day**, and you can sell your cars.

## Tuning

Parts use GT4 (USA) prices:

- Engine upgrades (turbo, NA tune, supercharger and more) and weight reduction
- Gearboxes with the final drive and every ratio
- LSD and brake balance
- Tyres for tarmac, dirt and snow
- Suspension kits
- Repaint in the car's factory colours


## Five cut tracks

Complex String, Smokey Mountain, Tahiti Dirt, Rome and Hong Kong were cut from GT PSP. You can race them in the career, Single Race and Time Trial, and they run on a real PSP.

## Installing

You need your own unmodified Gran Turismo PSP ISO: the USA version 1.00 or 2.00, or the European version 2.00. Open the [browser installer](https://memetrix.github.io/gran-turismo-psp-career/), choose your ISO and save the patched game. It works on computers and Android, and your ISO is never uploaded.

For a USA ISO take `Gran-Turismo-PSP-Career-v1.1.1.xdelta`. It works with all three known USA dumps of the game.

For a European ISO (UCES01245) take `Gran-Turismo-PSP-Career-v1.1.1-Europe.xdelta`. The career is translated into the disc's eight languages: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish, Dutch, Portuguese and Russian.

Manual installation with Delta Patcher or UniPatcher is covered in [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md). The patched game runs on a PSP with custom firmware and in PPSSPP.

The career has its own save and copies your garage and credits from your normal save, which stays untouched. Career saves from 1.1 load in 1.1.1; back up yours before updating.

## Moving your save from the USA version to the European one

The two versions keep their saves in different folders, and a PSP does not accept a folder that was only renamed.

In PPSSPP and on a PSP, start the European version while it has no save of its own. It finds your USA career save, tells you it has copied it, and saves to its own slot from then on. Your USA save is not changed.

On a PSP you can also copy a save in either direction with GT Career Save Converter. Unzip `GT-Career-Save-Converter.zip` from the [release page](https://github.com/Memetrix/gran-turismo-psp-career/releases/tag/v1.1.1), copy the `GTCSAVE` folder to `PSP/GAME` on the Memory Stick and run it from the Game menu. It asks before replacing a save that is already there.

## FAQ

**I chose 8 cars but I race 4 (or the Extended Grid row is greyed).**
Eight cars need the extra memory of a PSP-2000 or later. The PSP-1000 races four, and Città di Aria races four on every console.
- PPSSPP: Settings → System → PSP settings → PSP model must be PSP-2000/3000. Restart the game after changing it.
- PSP on custom firmware: on its first start the game asks ARK for the extra memory and tells you to restart. If the row stays greyed, open the CFW settings, change High Memory from Auto to Forced and start the game again.

**PS Vita / Adrenaline freezes, or the second eight-car race does not load.**
A player traced this to the "Suspend threads" option of Adrenaline Bubble Booter (Booter tab): turn it off. Plain Adrenaline has no such option.

**The game runs slowly with eight cars on my PSP.**
Choose 5 or 6 cars in Options → Extended Grid, or set the CPU clock above 333 MHz in your custom firmware settings.

## Known issues

- On a PSP, the frame rate drops on Rome and Complex String. For full speed, set the CPU clock above 333 MHz in your custom firmware settings.
- The cut tracks have small graphical glitches.

Bug reports: https://github.com/Memetrix/gran-turismo-psp-career/issues, with your console or PPSSPP version, the event and round, and what happened. Please don't post or ask for ISOs.
